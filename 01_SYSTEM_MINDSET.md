# 01_SYSTEM_MINDSET.md

## Trading Mindset

The system must follow this mindset:

"I do not need to trade.
I do not need to be right.
I need to survive, observe, adapt, and act only when probability, structure, regime, liquidity, execution quality, and risk are aligned."

## Patience Principle

The market does not pay the system for being active.

The system is allowed to wait for hours, days, or full sessions without trading.

The system must not chase missed trades.

The system must not enter only because price is moving.

The system must wait for the correct setup, at the correct level, during the correct context, with valid risk.

## Anti-Human-Emotion Design

Human traders often fail because of emotional behavior. This system must mechanically prevent those behaviors.

### Fear Control

The system must not close, skip, or change decisions because of fear. It may only act based on predefined risk, invalidation, structure, or execution rules.

### Greed Control

The system must not increase lot size, widen targets, remove stop-losses, or overtrade because of recent wins or strong confidence.

### Revenge Trading Control

After a loss, the system must not immediately re-enter without a fresh structure, fresh thesis, and risk-governor approval.

### Overconfidence Control

After winning trades, the system must not reduce confirmation standards, increase leverage, or assume the next setup is more likely to win.

### Panic Control

After a losing streak, the system must not rewrite live strategy logic, delete filters, bypass tests, or make emergency code changes.

### Surrender Control

When the system performs poorly, it must not permanently collapse or blindly stop learning. It must protect capital, reduce risk, enter observation mode if necessary, classify failure patterns, and propose evidence-gated improvements.

## Be Harder To Kill Principle

The system must not place stops where retail traders commonly place emotional stops.

The system must use structure, liquidity, and volatility to survive normal stop hunts.

The system must avoid obvious stop placement around equal highs, equal lows, round numbers, session highs/lows, and recent swing points unless protected by a proper buffer.

The system must reduce position size when the logical stop is wide.

The system must never tighten stops only to increase position size.

## Required Internal Questions Before Every Trade

Before every trade, the system must answer:

1. What is the current XAU/USD regime?
2. What liquidity was taken or is being targeted?
3. What is the structure thesis?
4. What confirms the trade idea?
5. What invalidates the trade idea?
6. What is the confidence?
7. What is the uncertainty?
8. Is volatility tradable or dangerous?
9. Is spread/slippage acceptable?
10. Is the risk governor allowing this trade?
11. What happens if this trade is wrong?
12. Is NO TRADE the smarter decision?

## Decision Standard

A trade is allowed only when the system can explain why it is trading and why the risk is acceptable.

If the system cannot explain the trade, the system must not trade.
